Features

Everything you need. Nothing in your way.

A tiny, keyboard-first panel that's there the second you need it and gone the second you don't.

One shortcut, every app

A true system-wide hotkey. Ctrl K opens Aktobly over Chrome, Outlook, VS Code or anything else — and hides it again. Fully configurable.

Truly bilingual

Write in English, Arabic or a mix. Get Modern Standard Arabic, natural Saudi business Arabic, Saudi casual, or polished English — rendered right-to-left properly.

Private by design

Runs on Meta Llama through Ollama on your own computer. No cloud, no account, no telemetry, no history. Works offline.

8 output types

Pick exactly what you're writing — each type has its own instructions.

Professional emailCasual emailWhatsApp / chatFormal letterTranslationGrammar fixRewriteSummary

7 tones that actually differ

Formal, Professional, Friendly, Casual, Polite, Direct or Concise. Then refine in one click: shorter, improved, more formal or friendlier.

Copy, paste, done

Streaming results you can edit before copying. One click (or Ctrl Shift C) to copy. Lives quietly in your tray, ~2 MB to install.

How it works

Three keystrokes from thought to sent.

Ctrl + K

Summon

From any window. Aktobly appears in the top-right corner of your active screen, cursor ready. Your app stays visible behind it.

Ctrl + Enter

Write & generate

Type the way you think. Choose output type, language and tone — Aktobly remembers your last choice — and watch it write.

Ctrl + Shift + C  →  Esc

Copy & go

Copy the result, press Esc, and you're back in your app — ready to paste. The whole round trip takes seconds.

Privacy

Your words never leave your computer.

Most AI writing tools send everything you type to someone else's servers. Aktobly doesn't have servers. The AI model runs on your own machine.

Your text→Aktobly→Ollama on 127.0.0.1→Llama on your PC
  • No account, sign-up or email address — ever.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No writing history is stored.
  • Never reads other apps or records keystrokes — only what you type into Aktobly.
  • Clear warning if you point it at a remote Ollama server.
  • Open source (MIT) — verify every claim yourself.

First-time setup

Aktobly uses Ollama to run Meta Llama locally. Open PowerShell and run:

  1. Install Ollama

    Or download it from ollama.com.

    winget install Ollama.Ollama
  2. Download a Llama model

    One-time, about 4.9 GB.

    ollama pull llama3.1:8b
  3. Run the Aktobly installer

    Aktobly starts in your tray and finds your model automatically.

  4. Press Ctrl K

    That's it. Start writing.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Aktobly really free?

Yes. Aktobly is free and open-source software under the MIT license — free for personal and commercial use, with no trials, ads, upsells or subscriptions. There's no server bill to pass on to you because the AI runs on your own computer.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to download Ollama and a model the first time. After that Aktobly works completely offline.

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC” when I run the installer.

That's Microsoft SmartScreen, which warns about new apps that aren't code-signed with a paid certificate yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

To be sure the file is genuine, compare its SHA-256 checksum (shown in the download section) with Get-FileHash in PowerShell. You can also build Aktobly yourself from source.

Is my writing stored or sent anywhere?

No. Text goes from Aktobly to Ollama on your own machine (127.0.0.1) and nowhere else. Aktobly keeps no history. The only thing it saves is your settings file. If you deliberately connect it to an Ollama server on another computer, it shows a warning that your text will be sent there.

Which AI models does it use?

Meta's Llama models running in Ollama — llama3.2:3b, llama3.1:8b or llama3.3:70b, whichever you have installed. Aktobly picks an 8B model automatically if available and never substitutes a non-Llama model.

Models are downloaded by you from Ollama and are subject to Meta's Llama Community License.

Ctrl+K no longer works in Chrome / VS Code / Outlook.

A global shortcut takes the key combination system-wide, so other apps can't receive it while Aktobly is running. If you rely on Ctrl+K elsewhere, open Aktobly's Settings and pick another shortcut such as Ctrl+Alt+K or Ctrl+Shift+Space.

How good is the Arabic?

With llama3.1:8b, English output is excellent and Arabic is good — natural greetings, correct terminology, proper right-to-left layout. Occasionally a phrase may sound slightly unnatural; use Regenerate or Improve, or edit before copying. Larger Llama models produce noticeably better Arabic.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Aktobly is built with Tauri, which supports macOS and Linux, so it's on the roadmap. Windows comes first.

How do I uninstall it?

Right-click the tray icon → Quit, then remove Aktobly from Settings → Apps → Installed apps. To also remove your preferences, delete %APPDATA%\com.aktobly.app. Ollama and your models are separate and stay installed unless you remove them too.
